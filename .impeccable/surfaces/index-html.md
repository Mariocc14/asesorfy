# Home de Asesorfy (empresas)

Scope: `index.html`, the new home. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: businesses, autónomos and property owners in Spain who need a legal text or an explanation, in their own words. Action: open a free tool (generators, contract reviewer, translator) or the right document or guide. Constraints: no prices on the home; never call it "asesoramiento jurídico"; the rental line stays reachable as a section.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a question desk. A large search field understands the need in the visitor's own words and takes them to a tool, document or guide. It turns down the usual hero with feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: The existing Asesorfy look: white and blue-grey ground, ink #0e1a2b, Asesorfy blue #1f5fa6 for actions, Fraunces headings, Inter interface, 10–14px cards with soft shadow. Matched words get a yellow highlighter.
STORY: The visitor types their problem, sees instant answers (tools first) and opens the free tool. Further down: the five tools, the rental line, the guides, and the stance "información, no asesoramiento".
FIRST VIEWPORT: Short centred headline. Full-width 64px search field with a live dropdown. Suggestion chips underneath. The row of tools shows just above the fold.
FORM: Structure #6 of 7, seed 917fb024. Signature interaction: live search, keyboard-navigable, with highlighted matches.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
